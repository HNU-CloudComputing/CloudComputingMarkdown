USER CONTEXT PROMPT

Source context from the textbook:
Before an Attempt starts, the scheduler checks and reserves model budget, sandbox lease, and trajectory-log writer. Only after all three services confirm reservation can the Attempt be marked running. If any step fails because budget is full, lease is unavailable, or log backlog is high, the scheduler releases already reserved resources and keeps the Attempt waiting.

Teaching goal:
Show a reversible admission transaction with clear coordinator/service roles: scheduler sends reservation requests, services confirm or fail, scheduler coordinates rollback, and only all confirmations allow commit.

Beginner misconception to prevent:
Do not show rollback as service-to-service cascade. Do not put backpressure next to successful commit. Do not make request and confirmation look like the same action.
