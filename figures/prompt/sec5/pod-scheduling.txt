Source context from the textbook:
The text explains that controllers decide what to do, such as creating a replacement Pod, but the Scheduler decides where the Pod should run. A Pod declares resource requests, and the Scheduler checks each Node individually. Nodes that cannot satisfy the full Pod requests are filtered out. Candidate Nodes are then scored by resource headroom and replica distribution, so same-deployment replicas are spread across different Nodes when possible.
