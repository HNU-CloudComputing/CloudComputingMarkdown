FIGURE CONTRACT

Why this figure exists:
The figure must show why a local transaction can preserve an official result and two delivery tasks together, while delivery to servers A and B proceeds independently after commit. It must make partial progress, pending state, retry, acknowledgment, and idempotent match checking visible in a consistent time order.

One teaching question:
After one local transaction commits match M1024 and two pending tasks, how can the system keep completed progress, retry the unconfirmed target, and avoid applying the same result twice?

Beginner misconception to prevent:
Students must not think the remote servers or Dispatcher participate in the local database transaction. They also must not think that one failed delivery rolls back another server that has already succeeded, or that retry means applying points twice.

Diagram role:
Three-stage state lifecycle plus mechanism path.

Required mechanism:
Stage 1: one local transaction commits the official result and two pending outbox tasks.
Stage 2: Dispatcher sends independently; server A succeeds and confirms, while server B returns no confirmation, so A becomes sent and B remains pending.
Stage 3: Dispatcher retries B; B checks M1024 before updating, returns confirmation, and both tasks become sent.
